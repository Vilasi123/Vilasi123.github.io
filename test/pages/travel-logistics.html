TRAVEL & LOGISTICS ARRANGEMENTS

Trusted partners, carefully chosen for every part of your celebration.

A beautiful wedding is built on the strength of the right partnerships. At Weddings by M&M, we bring together a trusted network of vendors—handpicked, vetted, and matched to your vision, budget, and style. From the first shortlist to the final handshake, our team manages every vendor relationship with clarity and care, so you can trust that every part of your celebration is in capable hands.

Vendor Selection & Shortlisting

We curate a shortlist of trusted vendors across every category, matched to your style, budget, and requirements, so you can choose with confidence.

Vendor Meetings & Coordination

Our team organises and manages vendor meetings, walkthroughs, and communication, ensuring every detail is aligned well ahead of your celebration.

Commercials & Negotiations

We handle vendor commercials and negotiations on your behalf, ensuring fair pricing, transparent terms, and the best possible value for every service booked.

Tour Planning & Customised Itineraries

We design personalised itineraries for your travel and your guests, blending celebration events with leisure and sightseeing.

Planning & Timeline Management

Our team builds a detailed travel timeline, coordinating bookings, transfers, and schedules so every journey stays on track.

On-Ground Vendor Coordination

We coordinate with travel vendors on the ground, managing transfers, drivers, and local partners so every journey runs smoothly.

Destination Wedding Travel & Guest Logistics

From group bookings to guest-wise travel plans, we manage the complete logistics of getting everyone to your destination smoothly.

Global Travel Planning

Our team coordinates international travel, visas, and documentation requirements, ensuring a smooth experience for you and your guests.

Budget-Based Travel Solutions

We tailor travel plans to fit your budget, sourcing the best options across flights, stays, and transfers without compromising comfort.

At Weddings by M&M, we believe seamless celebrations are built on strong partnerships. We manage every vendor relationship with diligence and care, so your celebration comes together exactly as envisioned.
