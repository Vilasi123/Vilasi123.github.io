Gifting & Hampers

Thoughtful gifts, beautifully curated, delivered with care.

A thoughtful gift is often the first impression a guest carries home from your wedding. At Weddings by M&M, we design gifting experiences that feel personal, elegant, and true to your celebration—from the first welcome hamper to the final thank-you note. Every hamper is curated with care, every gift chosen with intention, and every detail packaged and delivered so your guests feel genuinely valued.

Welcome Hampers & Guest Gifting

We design welcome hampers tailored to your guests' preferences and travel needs, combining local touches, personal notes, and thoughtful essentials to make every arrival feel special.

Custom Gift Curation & Packaging

Our team sources, curates, and packages every gift with an eye for detail—coordinating colours, materials, and presentation so each hamper reflects the look and feel of your celebration.

Family & VIP Gifting

From parents and close family to VIP guests and vendors, we curate distinct gifting tiers that honour every relationship, ensuring each gift feels personal and appropriately special.

Hamper Design & Styling

We design each hamper's look and feel to match your wedding's colour palette and theme, from basket styling to ribbon and tag details.

Sourcing & Vendor Coordination

Our team sources quality products from trusted vendors and artisans, coordinating orders, samples, and timelines well ahead of the celebration.

Personalised Tags & Notes

Every gift is finished with personalised tags, handwritten notes, or monograms, adding a warm and individual touch for each recipient.

Packaging & Presentation

From eco-friendly wraps to premium boxing, we ensure every hamper is beautifully presented and ready for the moment it's received.

Guest-Wise Delivery & Distribution

Our logistics team manages guest-wise sorting and room-drop delivery, ensuring every hamper reaches the right guest at exactly the right time.

At Weddings by M&M, gifting is more than a gesture—it is a reflection of gratitude, thoughtfully designed and delivered to make every guest feel truly cherished.
