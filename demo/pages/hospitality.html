Wedding Hospitality

Warm welcomes, thoughtful assistance, seamless guest experiences.

Exceptional hospitality is at the heart of every memorable wedding. At Weddings by M&M, we ensure that you, your families, and every guest feel welcomed, cared for, and supported throughout the celebration. From the first RSVP to the final farewell, our dedicated hospitality team manages every touchpoint with warmth, professionalism, and attention to detail—allowing you to enjoy your wedding while we take care of everything behind the scenes.

RSVP Management & Guest Relations

We manage guest confirmations, follow-ups, travel details, special requirements, and personalised event communication to ensure accurate planning and a smooth guest experience.

Guest Relation & Helpdesk

Our professionally managed hospitality desks assist guests with registrations, check-ins, event information, room allocations, transportation, and on-ground requirements.

Professional Shadows & Butlers

Dedicated personal shadows and butlers are assigned to support the couple, families, and VIP guests with their individual needs, ensuring personalised attention throughout the celebrations.

Luggage Management

Our dedicated luggage team coordinates baggage movement and arrival, ensuring the smooth transfer of belongings throughout the celebrations.

Runners & On-Ground Support

Our runners provide quick and efficient on-ground assistance, handling last-minute requests and supporting seamless coordination across wedding functions.

Hampers & Welcome Amenities

From welcome hampers and gift bags to personalised gifts, every item is carefully curated and delivered to the right guest at the right time.

Logistics & Guest Movement

Our logistics team manages guest arrivals, departures, and movement between venues to ensure timely and comfortable transitions.

Food & Beverage Management

Our hospitality staff manages guest catering, coordinating closely with catering and event teams so that every meal and beverage service runs seamlessly.

At Weddings by M&M, hospitality is more than a service It is about creating a thoughtful and effortless experience where every guest feels valued and every detail is taken care of.
