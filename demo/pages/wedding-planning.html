Wedding Planning

Your vision, our planning. One seamless celebration.

A wedding is a journey of countless moments, decisions, and details coming together to create one unforgettable celebration. At Weddings by M & M, we take care of every aspect of your wedding—from the first idea to the final farewell—ensuring that your journey is seamless, organized, and truly enjoyable. With over 10 years of experience, our team combines thoughtful planning, creative vision, meticulous execution, and flawless event skills to bring your vision to life. Whether it is an intimate celebration, a grand traditional wedding, or a destination event, we ensure a personalized plan that reflects your vision, style, and budget.

Concept & Wedding Planning

We understand your vision, preferences, and expectations to create a detailed wedding plan and roadmap tailored specifically to your celebration.

Budget Planning & Management

From hotel budgeting to booking expenses, we help allocate expenses efficiently while maintaining complete transparency throughout the planning process.

Timeline & Event Flow

We create detailed timelines for every function, ensuring that ceremonies, performances, entertainment, hospitality, and other key moments flow seamlessly.

On-Ground Management

Our experienced team is present throughout the celebration to coordinate every detail, manage vendors, prevent timeline delays, and handle last-minute requirements.

End-to-End Wedding Management

From venue selection, accommodation, décor, entertainment, invitations, gifting, and travel arrangements to complete on-ground execution, Weddings by M&M serves as your one-stop solution for wedding planning and management.

At Weddings by M&M, we believe exceptional weddings begin with exceptional planning. Our goal is simple—to take care of the details so you can be fully present, celebrate freely, and enjoy every moment of your wedding.
