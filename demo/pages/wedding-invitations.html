WEDDING INVITATIONS, COLLATERALS & DESIGN

Your celebration begins with the first impression.

Your wedding story begins long before the celebration—and your invitation is the first glimpse into the experience that awaits. At Weddings by M&M, we curate thoughtfully designed wedding invitations and personalised collaterals that reflect your story, style, and the unique identity of your celebration. From elegant traditional invitations to contemporary digital announcements, we ensure that every design element follows a cohesive visual language, beautifully connecting every touchpoint of your wedding.

Wedding Invitations

From classic printed invitations to bespoke invitation boxes, we design and curate invitations that set the tone for your celebration.

Digital & Video Invitations

Creative digital invitations, animated invites, and personalised video invitations designed for a modern and engaging guest experience.

Save the Dates

Beautifully designed digital and printed save the dates that give your guests the perfect first glimpse of your upcoming celebration.

Wedding Stationery & Collaterals

From welcome letters, itineraries, room keys, luggage tags and menu cards to table numbers, signage, and thank-you notes, we design every detail to create a consistent and seamless wedding experience.

Personalised Design & Branding

We create custom-tailored wedding logos, monograms, colours, and visual identities that can be carried across invitations, stationery, gifting, décor, and digital communication.

On-Site Signage & Guest Communication

From welcome signage and direction boards to event schedules and personalised guest communication, every element is designed to be both beautiful and functional.

Design, Production & Coordination

Our team manages the complete process—from concept and design to printing, production, packaging, and delivery—ensuring quality and consistency at every stage.

At Weddings by M&M, every detail tells a part of your story. We bring creativity, personalisation, and thoughtful design together to create invitations and collaterals that make your celebration memorable from the very first impression.
