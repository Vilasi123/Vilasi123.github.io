Photography & Videography

Capturing your story, one moment at a time.

Your wedding deserves to be remembered exactly as it felt—warm, joyful, and unmistakably yours. At Weddings by M&M, we bring together skilled photographers and filmmakers who capture every emotion, every glance, and every celebration in its truest form. From quiet, intimate moments to the grandest of festivities, our team is dedicated to preserving your story with artistry and care, so you can relive it for years to come.

Wedding Photography

Our photographers specialise in capturing every ritual and celebration with elegance and precision, blending traditional coverage with an artistic eye for detail and emotion.

Candid Photography

We focus on genuine, unposed moments—laughter, tears, and stolen glances—capturing the real emotions that make your celebration truly unforgettable.

Cinematic Wedding Films

Our filmmakers craft beautifully edited wedding films with cinematic storytelling, music, and pacing that bring your celebration to life on screen.

Pre-Wedding Shoots

Our team plans and shoots creative pre-wedding sessions at locations that reflect your story, style, and personality as a couple.

Drone Photography & Videography

Aerial coverage captures the scale and beauty of your venue and celebrations, adding a cinematic perspective to your wedding story.

Traditional & Live Coverage

We provide comprehensive traditional coverage of every ceremony and function, along with live streaming for guests who can't attend in person.

Photography Team Selection & Coordination

We handpick and coordinate the right photography and videography teams for your celebration, managing schedules and shot lists on your behalf.

Albums & Wedding Films

Every celebration is compiled into beautifully designed albums and edited wedding films, curated to relive your favourite moments for years to come.

At Weddings by M&M, photography and videography are more than documentation they are the art of preserving your story, so every moment can be relived exactly as it was felt.
