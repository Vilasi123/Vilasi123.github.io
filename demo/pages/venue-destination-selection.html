Venue & Destination Selection

Curating extraordinary venues for timeless memories

Choosing the perfect wedding venue is one of the most important decisions after finding your life partner. The venue sets the entire tone for your celebration—whether it’s a residential destination wedding or a single-event venue. From ambience and hospitality to services and guest experience, every detail plays a crucial role. Selecting a venue that aligns with your vision, offers seamless services, and fits within your budget ensures that your special day is not only beautiful but also effortlessly executed. Selecting the right venue requires a detailed evaluation to ensure it aligns seamlessly with your vision, logistics, and budget. At Weddings by M&M, we guide you through every aspect of venue selection, helping you make an informed and confident decision for your special day.

Venue Suitability & Availability

We assist in evaluating guest capacity, the overall ambience, and available dates that suit your timeline, ensuring the venue comfortably accommodates your guest flow. Availability for preferred dates and exclusivity of the property are carefully assessed.

Commercials & Inclusions

Our team helps you understand the complete pricing structure, including what's included with catering, décor, room blocks, and event spaces, so there are no surprises down the line and every inclusion is clear from the start.

Venue Suitability & Availability

We assist in evaluating guest capacity for outdoor spaces, seasonal considerations, and availability of alternate indoor arrangements, ensuring your preferred dates and exclusivity of the property align with your vision.

Food & Beverage Policies

We guide you through catering options—whether in-house or external—along with menu customisation, tastings, and liquor policies, including licensing, corkage, and service timelines.

Logistics & Infrastructure

From venue layout and function flow to power backup and technical capabilities, we ensure the space supports smooth execution. Curfew timings and operational guidelines are also taken into account.

Accessibility & Location

We evaluate the venue’s proximity to airports, railway stations, and key locations, along with ease of access, parking facilities, and overall convenience for all guests.

Contingency & Safety Measures

Backup options for weather, safety protocols, emergency preparedness, and medical support are carefully reviewed to ensure a secure and hassle-free event.

Vendor Management & Permissions

We coordinate vendor policies and entry logistics, ensuring smooth collaboration between the venue and all service providers.

Contractual Terms

Our team assists in reviewing payment schedules, cancellation policies, refund terms, and all contractual clauses to avoid any last-minute surprises.

A well chosen venue lays the foundation for a flawless wedding. With Weddings by M&M, every detail is thoroughly evaluated to ensure a seamless, elegant, and memorable celebration.
