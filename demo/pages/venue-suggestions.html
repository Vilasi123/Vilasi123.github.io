Venue Suggestions

Your perfect venue awaits — explore our hand-picked properties.

Rambagh Palace, Jaipur

Once the residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur, Rambagh Palace is among India's most iconic heritage venues — offering sprawling gardens, regal courtyards, and timeless royal grandeur for a truly majestic celebration.

ITC Rajputana, A Luxury Collection Hotel, Jaipur

Blending Rajasthani architecture with contemporary luxury, ITC Rajputana offers elegant banquet spaces, lush lawns, and impeccable hospitality — perfect for weddings that balance tradition with modern comfort.

Le Meridien Jaipur Resort & Spa

Surrounded by manicured gardens and domed architecture, Le Meridien Jaipur Resort & Spa offers a serene and elegant setting with spacious lawns and refined interiors, ideal for both intimate ceremonies and grand celebrations.

Fairmont Jaipur (Kukas)

Set against the Aravalli hills in Kukas, Fairmont Jaipur is a palace-inspired resort with expansive courtyards and sweeping views — a stunning backdrop for destination weddings that call for scale and splendour.

Le Meridien Jaipur Resort & Spa

With poolside lawns and elegant outdoor spaces, this property offers a versatile canvas for mehendi, sangeet, and reception functions alike, all wrapped in effortless five-star comfort.

Fairmont Jaipur (Kukas)

Fairmont Jaipur's grand facade and landscaped grounds create a striking setting for every wedding function, from a vibrant baraat entrance to an unforgettable reception under the stars.
